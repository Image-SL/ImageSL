Intended use

ImageSL measures the area of chromogenic staining in images of stained sections. It is intended for research, method development, quality control and teaching, and is designed to be used by people who understand the staining being measured and can judge whether a result is plausible.

It reports what it measured under the settings it was given. It does not interpret a result, and a number it produces is not a finding until a qualified person has decided it is one. Any result that will inform a conclusion should be reviewed by an appropriately qualified individual, and any result bearing on patient care must be confirmed by established, validated methods.

This build measures DAB only. Applying it to a different chromogen produces a number that is not meaningful.
