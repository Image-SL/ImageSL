What it does

A real measurement, not a guess at a thumbnail

Every number traces back to a pixel rule you can read in the source. No global intensity threshold — staining is measured as excess over a locally fitted background.

Automatic background removal

White-point, Otsu, chroma and texture evidence combine to find the tissue and throw away bare glass, haze and vignetting — even on wall-to-wall sections with no glass in frame.

Colour deconvolution

The chromogen is identified by its absorbance signature, not hue — so dense, near-black DAB is measured, not dropped, and a red counterstain reads zero.

Precise positive area

Each structure is measured at its own isophote, so area does not inherit intensity. A live sensitivity slider and focus / ignore regions re-measure instantly.

Batch & export

Drop a whole folder, then export one ZIP of images plus a CSV of every measurement — each figure on screen is the figure in the CSV, to the pixel.

Runs entirely offline

All analysis happens on your machine. No upload, no account, no slide ever leaves your computer — only a lightweight check for app updates touches the network.

Reproducible & open

The same code runs in the app and on the web. The method is documented and regression-tested on real slides and on synthetic scenes with a known answer.

How it works

Download, open, drop a slide

No setup. The app bundles the full analysis engine — install it and it works, with or without an internet connection.

⚕️ ImageSL assists interpretation. It is not a clinical diagnosis; results must be confirmed by a qualified pathologist. This build is DAB-only.
