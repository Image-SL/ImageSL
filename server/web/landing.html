Background removed first

The tissue is separated from bare glass, haze and lamp gradient before anything is measured, so an uneven field does not become signal.

Stain separated by absorbance

The chromogen is identified by its optical density signature rather than its hue, so dense deposits are measured instead of being discarded as dark.

Area you can check

Every figure on screen is the figure in the exported CSV, to the pixel, and the rule behind it is readable in the source.
